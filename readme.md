## 2. Describe the process our team uses  

Our team officially uses the **Scrumban** process — a hybrid method combining **Scrum’s structure** with **Kanban’s flexibility**.  
Initially, we worked with Plan–Build–Ship because it was simple and fast, but as the project evolved, we realized we needed better coordination, clearer progress tracking, and regular feedback.  
Therefore, we shifted fully to **Scrumban**, which still keeps the speed of Plan–Build–Ship while adding lightweight structure to manage work effectively.

### Process overview:
- **Planning phase:** Define the problem, identify inputs/outputs, and set goals for each member.  
- **Kanban workflow:** Use a visual board (*To Do → Doing → Review → Done*) to manage tasks transparently.  
- **Weekly review:** The team holds at least one meeting per week (about 10–15 minutes) to evaluate progress, and resolve any issues or blockers.
These short check-ins ensure that all members stay aligned, tasks remain on track, and adjustments can be made promptly to keep the project moving smoothly.  
- **Iteration:** Tasks are updated continuously — we don’t rely on fixed sprints but maintain small, manageable cycles.  
- **Final integration:** Merge components, test results, and prepare the final demo and presentation.  

**Reasons:**  
- Keeps the **speed and adaptability** of Plan–Build–Ship.  
- Adds **clear visibility** into who’s doing what through the Kanban board.  
- Encourages **continuous improvement** without heavy bureaucracy.  
It’s the perfect balance for a small, dynamic academic team.

## Further Thinking

### 1. How do Scrum, Kanban, Scrumban, and Plan–Build–Ship differ in terms of pace, flexibility, and roles?
- **Scrum** → structured sprints, defined roles, predictable rhythm.  
- **Kanban** → continuous workflow, maximum flexibility, no fixed roles.  
- **Scrumban** → combines Scrum’s discipline with Kanban’s adaptability.  
- **Plan–Build–Ship** → very fast and simple but lacks ongoing progress control.  

*In short:* Scrumban offers the **right balance** between flexibility and structure — ideal for a small team like ours.

Challenges:

**Scrumban** handles these perfectly because it provides a visual workflow and quick feedback without fixed sprint pressure.
